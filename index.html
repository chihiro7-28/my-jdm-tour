What We Do

The Tour
Experience

You choose up to 3 spots around Nagoya. I drive. You ride shotgun. Along the way, you'll hear the 1JZ roar, learn about drift culture, JDM history, and why the Chaser became a legend.

01

Nagoya Station

Meet at Nagoya Station. First stop — photos with the Chaser in the city.

02

Oasis 21

Oasis 21, the TV Tower, and the streets of Sakae. Stop, shoot, move on — the city is the backdrop.

03

On the Expressway

From the city center to the industrial waterfront. Just you, the road, and the 1JZ.

04

Meiko Triton

Arrive at the iconic bridge. Night shots of the Chaser with the illuminated Triton. Other JDM cars often show up too.

05

FamilyMart Stop

Stop at a convenience store on the way back. One drink on us — welcome to konbini culture.

06

Back to Nagoya

Back to Nagoya Station. End of the ride.

Book the
Experience

All bookings are managed through Airbnb for your security and convenience. Flexible cancellation available.

How many people can join?

The Chaser accommodates up to 3 guests. For groups of 1–2, guests rotate between the front and rear seat. For groups of 3, all guests ride in the rear — it's a cozy fit, so groups of 3 are available on private tours only.

Is English OK?

Yes! The tour is conducted in English. Japanese is also welcome.

Where does it start?

We meet at the Gold Clock (金時計) inside Nagoya Station — the most iconic landmark in the station. Easy to find, impossible to miss.

Can I take photos and video?

Absolutely — that's the point. Bring your camera, bring your phone.

Can I request dates not shown on Airbnb?

Monday through Thursday are not available. Fridays are available from 8:00 PM only. Saturdays and Sundays are flexible. Japanese public holidays may also be available. If you're not sure, just send us a message — we're happy to check!

Can I change the time after booking?

Yes, time changes are possible up to 2 days before the tour. Please send a message through Airbnb as soon as possible.
