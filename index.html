What We Do

The Tour
Experience

You choose up to 3 spots around Nagoya. I drive. You ride shotgun. Along the way, you'll hear the 1JZ roar, learn about drift culture, JDM history, and why the Chaser became a legend.

01

Nagoya Station

Meet at Nagoya Station. First stop — photos with the Chaser in the city.

02

Oasis 21

Oasis 21, the TV Tower, and the streets of Sakae. Stop, shoot, move on — the city is the backdrop.

03

On the Expressway

From the city center to the industrial waterfront. Just you, the road, and the 1JZ.

04

Meiko Triton

Arrive at the iconic bridge. Night shots of the Chaser with the illuminated Triton. Other JDM cars often show up too.

05

FamilyMart Stop

Stop at a convenience store on the way back. One drink on us — welcome to konbini culture.

06

Back to Nagoya

Back to Nagoya Station. End of the ride.
